# 纪念词

很高兴能够在 GitHub 上进行开发，与各位世界各地的开发者一同进行交流。我生于 2010 年 2 月，此文档著于 2026 年 9 月。我与在座各位开发大神还是有很大差距的。我还需要多加学习，追随前辈的脚步。RiscDom 是我的第一个大型项目，感谢与我并肩前行的朋友在制作路上给我带来的支持，也感谢每一位支持我们的人！

新的 RiscDom 是一个新的起点，我相信他一定能大有可为，希望在未来的路上，breakevery 可以成为一个开放的、非盈利性的开源内核研究组织，为开源内核的研究带来新的力量，breakevery 也随时欢迎每一位开发者加入。

目前来说，breakevery 会持续保持内核的纯洁性，我们的理念是开放内核不应该为商业化让步，谢谢大家！

zhanchenyi (from breakevery)
2026 年 9 月 21 日，写于中国湖北省

## 翻译内容

I am delighted to be developing on GitHub and to be exchanging ideas with developers from all over the world. I was born in February 2010; this document was written in September 2026. There is still a large gap between me and the veteran developers here. I have much more to learn, and I will follow in the footsteps of those who came before me. RiscDom is my first large project. My thanks go to the friends who walked alongside me and supported me along the way, and to everyone who has supported us!

The new RiscDom is a new starting point. I believe it will go far. I hope that, on the road ahead, breakevery can become an open, non-profit open-source kernel research organization, and bring new strength to open-source kernel research. breakevery also welcomes every developer to join us at any time.

For now, breakevery will keep the kernel pure. Our conviction is that an open kernel should not yield to commercialization. Thank you all!

zhanchenyi (from breakevery)
September 21, 2026, written in Hubei Province, China
